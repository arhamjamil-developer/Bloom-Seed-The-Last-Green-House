# Bloom Heroes: To Save Seed (The Last Greenhouse)

A solarpunk tactical lane defense and garden battler built in Unity (URP 2D).

Protect the last singing seed inside the Eden-9 greenhouse from Mother Rust's mechanical swarms across four regional relay gardens and endless survival expeditions.

---

## Overview

Years after the city gardens ran dry, high above the silent mechanical streets, one greenhouse still hums with life. Inside rests the Seed Core—the final surviving seed. When the seed begins to sing to the city's ancient irrigation relays, Mother Rust's automated rust swarm marches in to clear the pipes.

Join Pip (the watering-can caretaker) and four guardians to hold the lines, weave living botanical root chains, and restore the city's gardens.

---

## Core Features & Mechanics

- **Mobile Heroes & Rooted Plants:** Position anchored botanical allies to hold the soil, while stepping agile human heroes across open adjacent tiles to counter surprise lane breaches or dodge boss attacks.
- **Living Root Networks:** Cardinally adjacent plants connect their underground roots into unified chains, multiplying attack power across the connected network.
- **Bloom Merging (3-of-a-Kind Evolution):** Connect three matching plants of the same tier in a live chain to merge them into a single, high-tier bloom (+45% max HP, instant full heal, and +65% attack damage per tier).
- **Three-Phase Loop:**
  1. **Plan Phase (8s):** Read incoming lane warning chevrons, bank Sun income, and deploy or reposition units.
  2. **Defend Phase (Real-Time):** Dynamic combat across a 5x8 perspective grid.
  3. **Harvest Phase (Roguelike Upgrades):** Award Sun bounty and choose 1 of 3 deterministic upgrades (Sharp Shoots, Warm Roots, Sun Cache, Quick Growth, Living Armor).
- **Day / Night Cycle & Rechargeable Visor:** At night, daytime defenders fall asleep; manage the rechargeable Night Visor battery to wake your defenders when danger approaches.
- **Clutch Rescue ("Last Sprout"):** When the Seed Core drops to 3 HP or below, Pip provides an emergency +4 Sun boost and slows all enemies for 4 seconds.

## Roster

### The 4 Human Heroes (Mobile)
- **Dubu (Guard):** Front-line blocker with a broad blue barrier shield that counters on contact.
- **Bubu (Tether):** Clever tinkerer with an electric cobalt hair streak, slinging a frost rope reel to tether and slow advancing bugs (night-ready).
- **Arham (Shot):** Long-range scout with an orange scarf, firing rapid seed bursts up his lane.
- **Jojo (Mortar):** Spirited mechanic with copper locs, lobbing explosive 3x3 artillery charges from her shoulder mortar (night-ready).

### The 5 Botanical Companions (Anchored)
- **Cactus Guard:** Sturdy lane blocker returning thorn damage on contact.
- **Pea Pulser:** Rapid-fire lane sentry.
- **Lotus Frost:** Radiates icy waves along its lane, slowing down swarms (night-ready).
- **Mushroom Mortar:** Heavy artillery lobbing 3x3 spore blasts.
- **Sunflower Healer:** Restores health to adjacent damaged companions (night-ready).

## Game Modes

- **Story Campaign:** Four distinct relay garden chapters (*Dawn Nursery*, *Moonlit Canopy*, *Rustworks Gate*, *Seedheart Siege*) with illustrated storybook cutscenes.
- **Endless Expedition:** Scaled director curve, rotating seasonal climates, and late-run Blights (*Quick Swarms*, *Iron Shells*, *Sun Drought*).

## Technical Specifications

- **Engine:** Unity 6 / URP 2D
- **Target Platforms:** iOS (Universal portrait iPhone & iPad)
- **Resolution Support:** Responsive safe-area layout for notched iPhones and wide-aspect iPads.
- **Accessibility:** Independent sliders for Camera Shake and Screen Flash (0–100%) for visual comfort without loss of gameplay readability.
